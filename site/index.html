Every chat is end-to-end encrypted. Your server only ever stores scrambled data.

Most Matrix apps keep one message key for 100 messages or a week.

Vibe swaps it every 10 messages, or every hour.

So a leaked key unlocks almost nothing. We call it Strict encryption.

Each block of 10 messages gets a fresh key

No phone number.
No ads. No tracking.

  • Encrypted on your phone. Messages and files are locked before they leave your device.
  • No analytics, ever. Vibe doesn't count, profile or sell anything.
  • Quiet notifications. They carry only a room and message ID, never your text.
  • Your account, your choice. It lives on the Matrix server you pick, not with us.
