Every chat is end-to-end encrypted. Your server only ever stores scrambled data.

Most Matrix apps keep one message key for 100 messages or a week.

Vibe swaps it every 10 messages, or every hour.

So a leaked key unlocks almost nothing. We call it Strict encryption.

Each block of 10 messages gets a fresh key

Made for
real life.

  1. Locked chats

    Hide a chat behind your fingerprint. Long-press your picture to find it again.

  2. Send later

    Write it now. It arrives in their morning.

  3. Their time

    Their local time sits under their name, so you never text at 3 AM by accident.

  4. Disappearing messages

    Per chat, from an hour to 90 days.

  5. Low data mode

    Photos load only when you tap them. Uploads get smaller.

  6. Voice and video calls

    Encrypted calls inside the chat, with a floating window when you leave the app.

  7. Drag and drop

    Drag a screenshot from any app straight into a chat.

  8. Groups and communities

    Channels, invite links and public places to join.

No phone number.
No ads. No tracking.

  • Encrypted on your phone. Messages and files are locked before they leave your device.
  • No analytics, ever. Vibe doesn't count, profile or sell anything.
  • Quiet notifications. They carry only a room and message ID, never your text.
  • Your account, your choice. It lives on the Matrix server you pick, not with us.
