Vibe

Delete your account

Vibe has no accounts of its own. You sign in with a Matrix account, which lives on the Matrix server you chose (for example matrix.org or unredacted.org). Deleting it happens on that server.

In the Vibe app

  • Open Settings → Security & sign-in.
  • Tap Delete account at the bottom, read the warning, and type your Matrix ID to confirm. Your server may also ask for your password.

Your server then deactivates the account for good: you can't sign in again, and the username can't be used again. Vibe also asks the server to erase the messages you sent. (Servers that manage accounts on their own page, like matrix.org, may send you there instead; see below.)

Without the app

Use your server's own account page. On matrix.org: sign in at account.matrix.org and choose to deactivate the account. Other servers have a similar page, or you can sign in at app.element.io and use Settings → Account → Deactivate account. If your server has no such option, contact its admin.

What happens to your data

  • On the server: your profile, devices and sign-ins are removed. Vibe asks it to erase your messages too. Copies already delivered to other people's devices stay with them, as with email.
  • On your phone: signing out or uninstalling Vibe deletes everything Vibe stored on the device, including your encryption keys.
  • Vibe itself keeps nothing about you: it has no servers, no analytics and no tracking. See the privacy policy.
