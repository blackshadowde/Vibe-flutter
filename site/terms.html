Vibe

Terms of use

By using Vibe you agree to these terms. Vibe is a free, open-source app that works with the Matrix protocol. It has no servers of its own: your account and messages live on the Matrix server you choose, and that server's own terms apply too.

What you must not do

  • Share child sexual abuse material (CSAM) or anything that sexualises, grooms or endangers children.
  • Harass, threaten or bully people, or post hate speech.
  • Share illegal content, or use Vibe for fraud, spam, scams or malware.
  • Share someone's private information or intimate images without their consent.
  • Pretend to be someone else to mislead people.

Server admins can remove content and suspend accounts that break these rules or the law.

Report and block

  • Report a message: long-press it and choose Report.
  • Report or block a person: open their profile and choose Report or Block. Blocked people can't reach you.
  • Reports go to the admins of your Matrix server, who can act on them. In groups, admins and moderators can also remove messages and members.

Child safety

Vibe has zero tolerance for child sexual abuse and exploitation (CSAE). Anyone sharing CSAM or grooming children will be reported to the server's admins and to the authorities.

  • Report it in the app (see above), so the server admins can remove it and close the account.
  • Report it to the authorities as well: NCMEC CyberTipline (worldwide), cybercrime.gov.in (India), or your local police.
  • To reach Vibe's developer about child safety, report privately here. Reports are handled with priority, and Vibe follows the child-safety laws that apply to it.
