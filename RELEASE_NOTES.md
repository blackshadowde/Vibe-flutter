**Install fix**
- Vibe installs again on phones where Play Protect blocked it. Google blocks any downloaded app that can read notifications; Vibe only used that for the "Listening to" music status, so that feature is switched off for now

**Chats**
- Reply window: replying inside a conversation of replies opens it over the blurred chat with its own text box; photos, videos and files can be sent as replies too. Send, and it closes
- Dragged-in photos and files wait above the text box and go when you press send (with your text)
- Mentions look like the @name pill while you type
- Role tags (Owner, Admin, Mod) next to names in groups, and a group tag you pick under your profile
- Search members in groups and communities
- Group permissions are simply Everyone, Moderators or Admins
- Admins can delete a group or community

**People and sharing**
- Block someone from their profile
- Share my Vibe ID shows a QR code and shares a link that opens Vibe and starts a chat

**Account and security**
- Unlocking messages on a new device explains where to accept the request, with a Send again button
- Password and email open your server's account page (matrix.org and others)
- Security fixes from FluffyChat: pictures only load from your Matrix server, the recovery key copy button never opens the share sheet, resetting your key keeps your old messages
- iPhone: notifications use Vibe's own app group and close the database when done

**Android and web**
- The back gesture closes the open pane instead of the app
- Web: larger layout in wide windows, Vibe's name and colours in the browser, and an optional "Log out when the browser closes"
